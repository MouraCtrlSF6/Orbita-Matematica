Ferramenta: image_gen integrada.

## Prompt claro

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity reference image for the EXISTING Brazilian educational algebra game Órbita Matemática /sign profile selection page. This is a design prototype, not implemented code. Create one beautifully composed presentation board with a large desktop viewport at left (approximately 1440x900 aspect ratio) and a narrow mobile viewport at right (390x844 aspect ratio), both showing the SAME proposed design. No physical devices, no browser chrome, no external annotations.
Direction 1: light, calm and minimal, strictly based on existing design tokens. Background #f7f8fa, white cards, ink #26283b, muted #6a6e7b, primary purple #7860bc, pale selected lilac #f1ebfb, mint #aee3ce and peach #f1c3a8 only as small accents. Manrope bold headings and DM Sans body style. Card corner radius 16px, thin borders #e6e7ed, extremely subtle shadows. Spacious, polished, age appropriate for 8th grade without childish cartoon art.
ESSENTIAL: The existing brand is lowercase bold rounded wordmark "órbita" with small widely tracked "MATEMÁTICA" beneath. The accompanying thin-line orbital symbol is a round open circle crossed diagonally by an elliptical orbit, with a small plus in the center. Put a VERY LARGE low-opacity monochrome lilac orbital brand symbol centered in the BACKGROUND behind the complete main composition (watermark, decorative, low contrast), with the regular sized wordmark and symbol centered above the heading. Do not invent a new logo.
Centered heading exact text "Sua próxima descoberta começa aqui". Subtitle exact text "Escolha como entrar na expedição." Beneath, exactly TWO equally weighted SIMPLE white clickable profile cards, side by side desktop and stacked mobile. First purple outline book icon on soft lilac square, title "Sou professor", description "Organize salas e acompanhe seus alunos.", small purple arrow. Second purple outline rocket icon on pale mint square, title "Sou aluno", description "Entre na sua sala e explore as missões.", small purple arrow. Entire card is the action, no extra buttons inside and no forms. Preserve equal visual priority of two roles. Cards around 280x200 desktop, mobile 330x160 with generous spacing. Strong legible text. All main content fits mobile without clipping. Quiet background can have two thin orbital arcs and tiny mint/peach points in corners. No additional navigation, no login/email/password, no scores, timer or competition, no huge illustrations. Accurate Portuguese. Clean implementable flat UI.

## Prompt espacial

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate a polished high-fidelity alternative prototype of the /sign page for the existing Brazilian eighth-grade algebra game Órbita Matemática. One presentation board with a wide desktop viewport at left (1440x900 proportions) and a narrow mobile viewport at right (390x844 proportions). Show the SAME proposed interface responsive in both views. No devices, browser chrome, labels or annotations outside the UI.
Direction 2: restrained space identity from existing project, deep space background #090d20 and #131626 with sparse tiny stars, lilac orbital lines, flat design. Use white cards, primary purple #7860bc, pale lilac #f1ebfb, mint #aee3ce, peach #f1c3a8, white-ish text #edf2ff. Typography Manrope bold headings, DM Sans body. Minimal, calm, sophisticated but welcoming to teens. No photorealistic galaxies, no 3D objects, no glowing neon, no glassmorphism.
ESSENTIAL brand: lowercase rounded bold "órbita", small tracked "MATEMÁTICA" below, accompanying thin-line orbital logo (open round circle with diagonal elliptical orbit and plus sign at its center). Center a VERY LARGE subdued low-contrast lilac version of this brand SYMBOL in the PAGE BACKGROUND behind the central layout, at 7% opacity. Normal-sized lilac symbol and white wordmark centered near top of main composition. Symbol is decorative, must not hurt text contrast. Keep background quiet.
Exact heading "Pronto para a expedição?" centered in white. Exact subtitle "Escolha como entrar na expedição." in light muted lilac. Only TWO SIMPLE equally weighted clickable white cards, side by side desktop, stacked mobile, softly rounded 16px, thin lilac border. Card one: small purple outline book icon on pale lilac square, ink heading "Sou professor", muted description "Organize salas e acompanhe seus alunos.", purple arrow at lower right. Card two: small purple outline rocket icon on pale mint square, ink heading "Sou aluno", muted description "Entre na sua sala e explore as missões.", purple arrow at lower right. Entire card is action, no additional button. Generous whitespace and readable text. Main content fits mobile entirely. On desktop cards form centered compact group, not full width. A few small orbital points in mint and peach toward far edges. No extra navigation, forms, email, password, timer, competition, footer clutter or additional actions. Flat implementable web UI, accurate Portuguese.
